Polyclonal–Monoclonal Distance

Tracking
clonal dominance

PMD is a geometric framework for quantifying clonal dominance in integration site analysis, positioning clonal populations along a continuum from maximal evenness to monoclonality.

The PMD clonal plane

About the research

The PMD framework combines observed clonal richness and dominant-clone abundance to support interpretable comparisons of clonal composition. The research also investigates fragmented dominance arising from multiple vector integrations.
